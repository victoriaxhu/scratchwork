# Outcome

No counterexample can be in convex position. Altman (1963) proved that the vertices of a convex n-gon determine at least ⌊n/2⌋ distinct distances, and the regular n-gon attains exactly ⌊n/2⌋. A counterexample to the question must therefore be in non-convex position, so searches should start from non-convex configurations (for example perturbed lattice sections). Reference: E. Altman, On a problem of P. Erdős, Amer. Math. Monthly 70 (1963), 148–157.
