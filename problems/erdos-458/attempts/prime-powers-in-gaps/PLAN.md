# Plan: prime-powers-in-gaps

Reduce the inequality to prime powers inside a prime gap: the ratio lcm(1..p_{k+1}-1)/lcm(1..p_k) is the product of q over prime powers q^a (a ≥ 2) with p_k < q^a < p_{k+1}. Then (1) show a gap with a single prime power can never fail, and (2) enumerate all prime powers up to a bound X to test every gap below X exactly. A failure would need several prime powers packed into one gap.
